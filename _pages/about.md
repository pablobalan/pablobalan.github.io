Welcome to my website!

I am an Assistant Professor (Senior Lecturer) in the Department of Political Science at [Tel Aviv University](https://english.tau.ac.il/). I am also a [Research Affiliate](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/stigler/research/affiliate-fellows/current-cohort) at the [Stigler Center for the Study of the Economy and the State at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/stigler). I earned my PhD from the [Department of Government](https://gov.harvard.edu/) at [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu/) in November 2021.

I am a political economist studying developing countries, with a substantive focus on state capacity and social networks, a methodological focus on field experiments, and a regional focus on sub-Saharan Africa and Latin America. I study how informal institutions affect the prospects and implementation of programmatic reform. A first stream of my research studies the foundations of state-building in low-capacity settings using field-experimental methods. A second set of projects examines the political effects of family networks in various contexts, from promoting electoral accountability to facilitating political activism within firms. Overall, my work revolves around [the family, private property, and the state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Origin_of_the_Family,_Private_Property_and_the_State).

My work has been published or is forthcoming in the ***American Political Science Review***, the ***American Economic Review***, the ***Journal of Politics***, ***Comparative Political Studies***, and ***World Development***. Findings of my research have received coverage from [ProMarket](https://www.promarket.org/2023/07/24/family-ties-as-corporate-power/), [VoxDev](https://voxdev.org/topic/public-economics/can-low-capacity-governments-work-local-leaders-increase-tax-revenues), [EGAP](https://egap.org/resource/what-can-developing-countries-do-to-promote-urban-property-titling-lessons-from-the-dr-congo/), [J-PAL](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/subsidized-land-titles-social-institutions-and-land-formalization-democratic-republic), and the [International Growth Centre](https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Balan%20et%20al%20Policy%20brief%20March%202023.pdf). My public writing has appeared in [The Monkey Cage](/files/monkey-cage-arrow.pdf). My dissertation received [an honorable mention for the Ronald Coase Best Dissertation Award](https://www.sioe.org/news/coase-dissertation-award-2023-roya-talibova) from the Society of Institutional and Organizational Economics (SIOE). I am also a recipient of the [Alon Fellowship for Outstanding Faculty](https://che.org.il/en/scholarships-integration-outstanding-faculty/), awarded by the Israeli Council for Higher Education. At Harvard, I was affiliated with the Weatherhead Center for International Affairs ([WCFIA](https://wcfia.harvard.edu/)), the Institute for Quantitative Social Science ([IQSS](https://www.iq.harvard.edu/people/pablo-ezequiel)), and Evidence for Policy Design ([EPoD](https://epod.cid.harvard.edu/)). During 2020–2021 I was a visiting scholar at the [Penn Development Research Initiative](https://pdri-devlab.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania.

Before pursuing graduate studies at Harvard, I co-authored a general-audience [book](https://lccn.loc.gov/2014510963) on political development in Argentina, my home country. The essay won a [national book award](/files/book-prize-lanacion.pdf) and was declared of public interest by the Legislature of the City of Buenos Aires. Here is a [short profile](/files/profile-lanacion.pdf) in La Nación, Argentina's leading newspaper.
